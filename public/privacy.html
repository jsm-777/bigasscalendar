Privacy policy

Big Ass Calendar · Last updated 2 October 2026

Big Ass Calendar is a personal planner used by a small, invite-only group. It shows and edits your own Google Calendar events, Google Tasks, and a small settings file in your Google Drive.

What the app accesses

  • Google Calendar: to show, create, edit and delete your events.
  • Google Tasks: to show, create, edit and complete your to-dos.
  • Google Drive app data: one private file (big-ass-calendar.json) in a hidden app folder that holds your goals, check-ins, notes and display preferences. The app cannot see any other Drive files.
  • Your name and email: to show who is signed in and to check you are on the allowed list.

Where your data is stored

Your data stays in your own Google account. The app's server does not store your events, tasks or notes. It keeps only an encrypted sign-in cookie in your browser so you stay signed in. Your browser talks to Google directly.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising. Big Ass Calendar's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Sign out in the app's menu, or remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing access does not delete your Google Calendar events or Tasks.
